# Match End Time

With the Match end time feature, you can manage all your services easily and efficiently, without worrying about forgetting to renew. With just a few clicks, you can set the expiration date for all your accounts, saving time and effort.

**Steps:**

1. **Log in to your account:**
   * Go to the vConsole page at: [https://dashboard.console.greennode.ai/](https://dashboard.console.greennode.ai/).
   * Follow this guide to log in to your GreenNode account here.
2. **Go to the resource management page:**
   * Find and select "Resource statistics", or go to the following link: [https://dashboard.console.greennode.ai/resource](https://dashboard.console.greennode.ai/resource).
   * You will see a list of resources currently in use across all services.
3. **Select the resources to adjust:**
   * **Step 1: Find the resources to adjust**
     * Filter by Product and the Resource Type belonging to that product
     * Filter by Renewal Type:
       * Manual: Resources without auto renewal enabled
       * Auto-Renewal: Resources with auto renewal enabled
     * Filter by Billing Status
       * Good: Resources with more than 15 days remaining
       * Warning: Resources with 5 to 15 days remaining
       * Alerted: Resources with less than 5 days remaining
       * Expired: Expired resources pending deletion
   * **Step 2: Check the box next to the name of each resource you want to adjust.**
     * Alternatively, you can select "Select all" to adjust all resources.
   *   **Step 3: Select the Match end time action**

4. **Set the new end date:**
   * Step 1: Select the new expiration date and time to apply to the resources
   * Step 2: Review the additional cost for each resource, corresponding to the extended period
   *   Step 3: Click Match end time to confirm the renewal

5. **Complete the payment on the payment page to finish the process:**

**Note:**

* **Resource types:** This feature applies to the following resources: Resources with Billing = Prepaid
* **Additional costs:** Adjusting the end date incurs additional costs, similar to renewing a resource
