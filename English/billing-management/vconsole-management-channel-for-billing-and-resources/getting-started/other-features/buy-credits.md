# Buy Credits

To top up credit in your GreenNode Service wallet, follow these steps:

* Step 1: Go to the vConsole page here: [https://dashboard.console.greennode.ai/](https://dashboard.console.greennode.ai/)
* Step 2: Click **Available credit** in the **Header** to view your wallet information
* Step 3: Click **Buy Credits** to top up credit in your wallet
* Step 4: In the popup that appears, **select the amount you want to top up**, then **click Buy** to be redirected to the payment gateway
* Step 5: At the payment gateway, **select the payment source** to use for the top-up, then **click Confirm payment**
* Step 6: Follow the instructions for your selected payment source
* Step 7: Once the payment is complete, **return to vConsole**, go to **Available credit** to view your balance, and go to **Credit history** to view balance changes.
